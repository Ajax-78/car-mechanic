
Car Battery Problems

A car that does not start can have several causes.

Common battery-related symptoms include:
- Clicking sound when starting
- Dim headlights
- Slow engine cranking
- Electrical accessories not working
- Complete loss of electrical power

A weak or discharged battery can prevent the starter motor from operating correctly.

Possible causes include:
- Discharged battery
- Old battery
- Corroded battery terminals
- Faulty alternator
- Electrical drain

If the vehicle repeatedly requires a jump start, the battery and charging system should be inspected.
