
Brake Problems

Grinding noises during braking can indicate severely worn brake pads, damaged brake rotors, or foreign material between the brake pad and rotor.

Common brake symptoms include:
- Grinding noise
- Squeaking
- Brake pedal vibration
- Vehicle pulling while braking
- Reduced braking performance

Grinding is more concerning than a light squeaking noise because it can indicate that brake components are being damaged.

If braking performance is significantly reduced, the vehicle should not be driven until inspected by a qualified mechanic.

Possible causes include:
- Severely worn brake pads
- Damaged brake rotors
- Foreign material
- Sticking brake caliper

A mechanic should inspect the brake pads, rotors, calipers and related components.
