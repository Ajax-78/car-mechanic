
Engine Overheating

Engine overheating can be caused by problems in the cooling system.

Common causes include:
- Low coolant
- Coolant leaks
- Faulty thermostat
- Cooling fan failure
- Radiator problems
- Water pump problems

Common symptoms include:
- Temperature gauge reaching the red zone
- Steam or smoke from the engine compartment
- Coolant smell
- Warning light
- Reduced engine performance

If the engine is severely overheating, the vehicle should be stopped safely and the engine allowed to cool.

Do not open the radiator cap while the engine is hot because pressurized coolant can cause serious burns.

Professional inspection may be required if the overheating continues.
